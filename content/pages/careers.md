---
title: Careers & Open Call
intro: We grow as a collective, not a payroll.
---
Immersive People is built as a network of curators, artists, storytellers, researchers, technologists and production partners. Rather than posting fixed vacancies, we run a continuous open call.

**Who we look for.** People with a subject area they know deeply (heritage, science, nature, urban culture, mythology), a creative craft (sound, visual, spatial, narrative design) or a strategic capability (curation, licensing, production management, partnerships).

**How collaboration works.** Depending on the project, collaboration can mean co-creating a new production, adapting an existing one for a venue or market, contributing research or craft to a production in development, or representing the collective in a region.

**How to apply or nominate.** Send a short note to hello@immersivepeople.co with who you are, what you make or know, and one thing you would love to see become an immersive experience. Links beat attachments. We read everything and reply to conversations we can act on.

**Institutions and venues.** If you are a venue, museum or institution looking to host or commission work rather than join the collective, request a deck through the same address.
