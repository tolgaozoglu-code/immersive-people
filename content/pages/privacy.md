---
title: Privacy Policy
intro: How Immersive People handles information on this website.
---
**Who we are.** Immersive People is a multidisciplinary collective creating and licensing immersive productions. For anything related to this policy, contact us at hello@immersivepeople.co.

**What we collect.** This website does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information to browse it. When you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to share (your name, email address and the content of your message) and use it only to respond and to manage our working relationship with you.

**Hosting and technical data.** The site is hosted on Netlify. Like most hosting providers, Netlify keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and operational purposes. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.

**Cookies and analytics.** The public website does not set marketing cookies and does not run advertising trackers. If we introduce analytics in the future, this policy will be updated first.

**Sharing.** We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only with service providers strictly necessary to operate the site (such as our hosting provider) or where required by law.

**Retention and your rights.** We keep correspondence only as long as needed for the purpose it was shared. You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete information you have sent us by writing to hello@immersivepeople.co.

**Changes.** We may update this policy as the site evolves. The current version is always published on this page.
